One academic accomplishment I am proud of is winning the Best Student of the Year for Lower Secondary.

I achieved it by trying my best throughout the whole school year, getting good grades consistently, and participating in multiple activities and events that helped with building my reputation.

Winning this award felt truly special to me, because not only was I chosen as the Year 8 candidate, but I was also selected among the candidates from Year 7 and Year 9 for the Best Student of the Year for Lower Secondary.

However, there are still some parts that I need to improve in to become even better in the future. A few academic areas I want to continue improving are focus and leadership. Moving forward, I want to stay more focused during lessons, talk less, and ultimately take part in more activities as the role of a leader.

Growth

My Growth

Then

I used to struggle a lot with focusing, and the fact that I spent around 2–3 years attending online school didn't help. During that time, I constantly got distracted by things outside of the screen and often lost focus during class.

Now

After transferring back to campus school, I have definitely improved in terms of focus and productivity. However, I still sometimes catch myself talking about unrelated things in class, so there is still room for improvement.

Next

As my future gets closer and closer, I will definitely need to make better use of my time and focus more during lessons. If I want to get into a prestigious university or get a good education in general, I need to decrease the time I spend on unproductive activities and increase the time I spend studying.

Reflection

Final Reflection

Over the course of my lifetime, I have always attempted to learn from the mistakes I made. From my early secondary years, all the way to Year 9, I have learned a lot of valuable things about building my social life and improving my studies.

I used to be afraid of taking charge - I was never really known as a good leader. However, after slowly building up my confidence over the years, I try my best to take charge when I can, take responsibility for criticism and feedback, and most importantly, learn how to communicate and collaborate well with others.

I'm usually satisfied with my academic results, and it makes me happy seeing how proud my parents are. However, I always feel like I can do better and that there's someone who will always be better than me. I have met people who are more productive and academically outstanding than me, and truthfully, a part of me does feel envious, but rather I take them more as inspiration to try harder.

People say comparison is the thief of joy, but I personally believe it's one of the only ways you'll change for the better.
